## 4. Method in one paragraph
Each Overture road segment lists connectors (id, position along the line). Consecutive connectors define graph edges ("pieces"). Nodes on motorway/trunk/primary/secondary pieces (and dead-end nodes within 200 m of the region boundary)
form the sink. A depth-first search from a virtual sink finds all bridges (edges whose removal disconnects part of the graph); a building snapped to node v is "single-route" if a bridge lies on its path to the sink and its "load" is the number of
dwellings in the subtree behind the TOPMOST bridge. Dwellings: ACS B25001 housing units of the tract spread equally over residential-candidate buildings (area >= 35 m2, no commercial/industrial/agricultural/outbuilding tags);
area-weighted and structure-count versions are reported as sensitivities. S30 = single-route dwellings whose load >= 30 (IFC Appendix D D107.1).
