## Why the scorecard cannot see it (metric blind spot, by definition)
Official evaluation text (challenge page, section Evaluation): the road component is Overture road LENGTH per tract vs Census TIGER,
"named-highway classes only: TIGER MTFCC S1100/S1200 against Overture motorway, trunk, primary, secondary".
(1) A single-link bottleneck is by construction upstream of the arterial network: the egress search ends when a path reaches any motorway/trunk/primary/secondary
    node, so the choke links are local roads (residential, unclassified, tertiary) that the score never measures. Observed: 0.0% of 10,951 segment-km of single-egress links lie in those classes (81% residential, 11% tertiary, 8% unclassified) although the classes are 17.9% of all drivable km. 97.9% of single-link dwellings live in tracts that DO contain scorecard-class road (tables/s30_in_tracts_without_scorecard_roads.csv): their road score is defined, but computed from arterials only.
(2) A length ratio carries no topology: removing 30 m of connector from a graph changes a length score by ~0 and changes the access of thousands of dwellings.
(3) The scorecard's strata (urban/rural, SVI, CVI, tribal, heat/wildfire/drought) do not include housing type: seasonal and mobile-home communities are cut across all of them.
